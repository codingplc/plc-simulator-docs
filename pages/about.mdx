# About

PLC Simulator Online brings the power of Programmable Logic Controller (PLC) coding to your fingertips. Our platform provides a unique, interactive experience, bridging the gap between theory and practice.

The simulator lets you code, simulate, and share Ladder Logic diagrams directly in your browser — no hardware, no setup. It started in 2020 as a learning tool and has grown into one of the most-used free PLC simulators on the web.

### A Growing Community

PLC Simulator Online is used by 250,000+ students, hobbyists, and engineers every year, across 190+ countries. Together they have created and shared more than 95,000 saved projects.

### Open-Source

In August 2024, PLC Simulator Online was released as an open-source project, inviting developers worldwide to contribute. Read the [announcement](https://www.codingplc.com/blog/plc-simulator-online-goes-open-source) or visit the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/codingplc/plc-simulator).
